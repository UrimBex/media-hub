What Kosovo has, and what it doesn't

Kosovo has fewer free on-demand film services than EU countries. I found no confirmation that Pluto TV, Tubi, the Roku Channel or Rakuten TV's free section officially serve Kosovo. RTVE Play, BBC iPlayer and most of ARTE are locked to their own countries or to the EU. JustWatch's Kosovo pages do list its new free service, JustWatch TV (beta), so it is worth a look. What works well: live TV from Kosovo and Albanian broadcasters, archives on their official YouTube channels, and worldwide services like the Internet Archive, NFB and NASA+. For any specific film, search JustWatch Kosovo with the Free filter.

Before you press play

Free means ads

No subscription, but most services show ad breaks similar to broadcast TV. Public broadcasters carry fewer or none.

Catalogues rotate

Licensed films come and go every month. Public broadcasters keep many programmes for a limited window, often 30 days to a year.

Country locks are real

Services check where you connect from. Using a VPN to appear in another country breaks most services' terms, so this atlas lists what is officially offered in each country.

Free next to paid

Rakuten TV, Atresplayer, ITVX, Joyn and others mix free and paid titles. Look for the "Free" or "Gratis" label or filter before pressing play.
