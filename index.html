Your free, legal media center

Seven tools in one place: a launcher for free streaming, a Jellyfin library for the films you own, a music player, a media player with subtitles, a guide to free TV in your country, a learning catalogue, and a script that organises your files automatically.

Favorites, playlists, history and resume positions are saved in this browser only. To use the hub on a TV or phone, open it from GitHub Pages or from the laptop's local server (see the README).
